About Me

I am a 28-year-old IT professional with approximately five years of experience working as an IT Support Engineer at Amazon. I was born and raised in Rome, Italy, and I am of Nigerian heritage. At the age of 18, I moved to Glasgow, Scotland, to further my education. I initially planned to study Law at the University of Glasgow; however, I quickly realised that I was naturally more drawn to technology. As a result, I began independently studying IT-related subjects and working towards industry-recognised certifications, including CompTIA A+, CompTIA IT Fundamentals+, Cisco CCNA, and several AWS certifications. Earning these certifications helped me transition from a full-time Amazon Warehouse Associate to an IT Support Associate. I continued developing my technical knowledge and skills until I was eventually promoted to my current role. I am now seeking an opportunity to bridge the remaining skills gap and transition into a more technical position within the company. My long-term goal is to become a Software Development Engineer within a FAANG company. I recognise that this is not an easy path, particularly given my unconventional entry into the technology industry and the rapid investment being made in artificial intelligence. Nevertheless, I am committed to achieving this goal. I am currently enrolled in a Full-Stack Developer Bootcamp offered by Codecademy and funded by Amazon, which is helping me build the technical skills required to take the next step in my career.
